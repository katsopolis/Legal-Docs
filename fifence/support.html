FiFence Support

FiFence is a local-first subscription and recurring-payment tracker developed by Lorewound. If you need help with the app, contact us at support@lorewound.com.

When Contacting Support

Please include:

  • Your FiFence version and build number from the bottom of Settings
  • Your device model and operating-system version
  • A short description of what happened and the steps that led to it
  • A screenshot or screen recording when it helps explain the issue

Do not send passwords, full card numbers, CVV codes, recovery codes, or other sensitive financial information.

Purchases and FiFence Pro

Purchases, billing, cancellation, and refunds are handled by the app marketplace associated with your device. You can restore an existing FiFence Pro purchase from Settings > Subscription.

Account and Data Deletion

FiFence works without an account. If you signed in for cloud sync, open Settings > Account > Delete Account to permanently delete your account and cloud-synced data. Locally stored data can be removed by resetting app data or uninstalling FiFence.
